A drop of pond water

Eight microbes · one drop

A whole ecosystem, smaller than a pinhead.

Everything in this chamber is real: viruses that hunt bacteria, a bacterium that swims with a rotary motor, the toughest animal known, and the yeast that bakes bread. They drift together here at similar sizes so you can compare them — the true sizes are listed for each one. Click any microbe in the water, or pick one from the list, to take a closer look.

Did you know? A single drop of pond water can hold thousands of micro-organisms — and most of them, like most of these eight, are completely harmless to us.
