Pick an exhibit from the switcher on the right and it takes the stage alone, large in the dark. The telescopes and supernova remnants are NASA’s own 3D models, downloaded the first time you choose them; the galaxies are rendered live as particle systems of tens of thousands of stars, because no model file of a galaxy exists — only maps like this.

Did you know?

NASA publishes its 3D models free for anyone — the same archive behind this wing also flies in MissionVerse, one gallery over.
