An Interactive 3D Museum

The Unusual Museum

Thirteen galleries of things you can spin, zoom and take apart in your browser: from a single living cell to the edge of the observable universe, by way of creatures that never existed, dinosaurs, gemstones, real NASA spacecraft, the galaxies themselves and a bowl of lentils. Every exhibit is real 3D: drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, click anything that catches your eye.

Mythos Museum

Gallery III: Creatures That Never Existed

The Abyss

Gallery IV: Deep-Sea Specimens

Three specimens in glass tanks, 1,800 metres down: the glowing anglerfish, the unclosable fangs of the viperfish, and a dumbo octopus flapping its ears through the dark.

3 specimens

Cosmos

Gallery V: Solar System & Stars

The Sun and all eight planets on their orbits: ride along with Earth, count Saturn's rings, then visit twelve famous stars standing next to the Sun, for scale. Betelgeuse is rude.

8 planets, 12 stars
VI

Elementum

Gallery VI: The Periodic Table

All 118 elements as minted 3D tiles in the classic table. Pick any one and open its atom: a nucleus built from its actual protons and neutrons, electrons orbiting in their real shells.

118 elements

Gallery VII: The Garden

A sunlit greenhouse market: every kind of lentil in heaped bowls, eighteen vegetables in their crates, and twelve leafy greens swaying in the beds, each with its nutrition and its best dishes.

40 exhibits

DinoVerse

Gallery VIII: The Prehistoric Hall

Eight giants in a sandstone hall: a T. rex with its jaw open, a stegosaurus wearing its plates, a brachiosaurus grazing the ceiling beams, and a pteranodon circling above them all.

Every James Webb model NASA ever released, the supernova remnant Cassiopeia A in three generations of data, six great galaxies built star by star, and Sagittarius A*, the black hole at our galaxy's heart.

Built with Three.js: real-time 3D

Every exhibit is rendered live in your browser, not a video, not a picture. If it spins, it's real geometry.

A flying dragon, an anglerfish and more, from real artists under CC0 & CC BY. Full credits in CREDITS.

Works on any device

Phone, tablet or desktop: drag, pinch and tap your way through all seven wings, no installs, no tickets.
